2. Principle: no cookies, no tracking

This website is a purely static information page. It uses:

  • no cookies,
  • no analytics or tracking tools,
  • no advertising networks,
  • no contact forms,
  • no sign-up and no user accounts.

No cookies are set and no information is stored on your device. A cookie banner is therefore not required.

No embedded third-party content

Every part of this page — fonts, images and scripts — is served exclusively from our own web space. In particular, no Google Fonts, no CDNs and no external image services are embedded. Opening the page therefore makes no connection to third-party servers, and your IP address is not passed on to third parties.

The page contains links to external services (e.g. Unsplash, the App Store, Google Play). These are only contacted when you actively click such a link. From that point on, the privacy policy of the respective provider applies.

When you visit the website, the host automatically collects and stores information that your browser transmits, in so-called server log files. This usually includes:

  • the IP address of the requesting device,

This data is technically necessary to deliver the website and to ensure its stability and security. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in the secure and reliable operation of the website).

The transfer is based on this adequacy decision under Art. 45 GDPR.

4. Retention

We do not store any personal data ourselves in connection with your visit to this website. How long server log files are kept is determined by the host.

If you contact us by email, we store your enquiry, including any personal data it contains, for as long as necessary to handle it, or for as long as statutory retention periods require. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (pre-contractual measures) or Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

5. Your rights

You have the right at any time to:

  • access the personal data stored about you (Art. 15 GDPR),
  • rectification of inaccurate data (Art. 16 GDPR),
  • erasure (Art. 17 GDPR),
  • restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR),
  • data portability (Art. 20 GDPR),
  • object to processing (Art. 21 GDPR).

To exercise these rights, please use the contact details above.

Right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority

Without prejudice to any other remedy, you have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the EU member state of your habitual residence, your place of work or the place of the alleged infringement (Art. 77 GDPR).

6. SSL/TLS encryption

For security reasons, this site uses TLS encryption. You can recognise an encrypted connection by the browser's address bar showing https:// instead of http://.

7. Changes to this privacy policy

We will update this privacy policy whenever changes to the website or to the legal situation require it.
