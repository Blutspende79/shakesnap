✦ What you get

Everything an instant photo needs —
and more.

Instant Photo Frame

Snap with the live camera or pick a shot from your gallery. It drops straight into a classic white instant-photo frame — wide bottom border and a soft shadow, so it almost feels real. Front or back camera, pinch to zoom, tap to focus.

Write & Doodle

Type a caption in a handwriting-style font or grab the digital pen and draw right on the white border — in any color you like. The border is your canvas.

Share Anywhere

Send your instant photo to friends on WhatsApp, Signal or any other messenger — or post it straight to Instagram and your other socials. You can also save it to your gallery in high resolution.

✦ The surprise feature

Send a mystery.
Gift a moment.

Send a friend an undeveloped instant photo — just black film. They have to shake their phone to bring it to life: the harder they shake, the faster it develops. Stop, and it freezes mid-reveal.

Perfect for birthday surprises, vacation postcards, or trolling your friends with a photo of your lunch.

"is it a selfie? a meme? a vacation dump?

✦ How it works

Three steps.
Endless fun.

1. Snap or pick

Take a photo in the app or choose one from your gallery. It instantly appears in an instant-photo frame, ready for your personal touch.

2. Add your touch

Write a caption or draw on the white border. Then decide: share it fully developed, or send it undeveloped as a surprise.

3. Send the vibe

Share via WhatsApp, Signal, Instagram or save to your gallery. Your moment, your call.
